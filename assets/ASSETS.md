# Website imagery

The existing `team-dog.webp` and `team-beach.webp` are the original repository photographs. The original logo, advert, background and price-guide assets are retained.

`brand-mark.webp` is an optimised copy of the original brand logo.

`groomed-dog.webp` (1120 × 1400) and `groomed-dog-small.webp` (560 × 700) are responsive copies of a new decorative hero portrait, created with the built-in image-generation tool. The portrait is not presented as a customer photo or a photograph of the premises.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: dog grooming website hero photograph. Create a premium editorial pet portrait for Barking Mad Barbers, a friendly dog grooming business, with absolutely no text, logos or graphics. A beautiful apricot cockapoo with freshly groomed soft curly fur, natural dark brown eyes, black nose and a very subtly happy open mouth. Seated in a relaxed pose, upper body and front paws visible, looking directly at the camera, head slightly tilted. Simple muted warm olive-brown studio background and matching floor, beautifully diffused warm daylight from upper left, realistic finely detailed fur, warm earthy photographic colour grading. The dog is large and central with breathing room above ears and around paws. Vertical portrait aspect ratio approximately 4:5. Beautiful authentic commercial photography, 85mm lens, not cartoon, not overly glossy, no collar, no props, no people.
