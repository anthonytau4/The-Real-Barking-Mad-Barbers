# Website imagery

`hero-advert.png` is the original Barking Mad Barbers grooming advert. It is restored as the homepage and sanctuary image, displayed without cropping or overlaid labels.

`premium-white-gold-background.webp` is the original site backdrop, restored through `site-background.css`.

`team-dog.webp` and `team-beach.webp` are the original repository photographs. The original logo and price-guide assets are retained.

`brand-mark.webp` is an optimised copy of the original brand logo.
